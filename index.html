Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is your typical project timeline?

Our timelines depend heavily on the project scope. However, for a standard enterprise web application or MVP, we typically deliver the first iteration in 36h on average. We utilize agile methodologies to ensure you see continuous progress.

Do you offer post-launch support?

Absolutely. We offer dedicated Service Level Agreements (SLAs) ranging from standard bug fixing to proactive 24/7 monitoring, infrastructure scaling, and ongoing feature development to turn your product into a living, evolving ecosystem.

How do you handle data security and compliance?

Security is baked into our architecture from day one. We implement Zero-Trust principles, end-to-end encryption, and follow best practices for SOC2, HIPAA, or GDPR compliance depending on your industry requirements.

Can you integrate AI into our existing legacy systems?

Yes. A major part of our offering is enterprise modernization. We can wrap your legacy systems in secure modern APIs and integrate intelligent agentic workflows or predictive analytics without requiring a complete rewrite of your core infrastructure.
